About Us

Rayyan Enterprises exists to make retiring electronics simple, secure and worthwhile — for the businesses that generate them and the planet that absorbs them.

Our Story

From scrap yards to a standard.

Rayyan Enterprises started with a simple observation: most organisations treat retired electronics as a liability to get rid of, not an asset to recover. We built a buying process that flips that — fast valuations, transparent pricing and a paper trail you can actually use for compliance.

Today we work with IT teams, facility managers, hospitals, schools and individuals across India, moving thousands of devices a month through certified, traceable channels instead of the informal scrap economy.

Our mission

Make responsible e-waste disposal the easiest option, not the hardest one.

Our approach

Pay fair value first, then handle certified destruction and recycling end-to-end.

Our standard

Every pickup is documented, every drive is destroyed to a verifiable standard.
