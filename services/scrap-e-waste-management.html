Scrap & E-Waste Management

We buy, lift and manage scrap of all types — including e-waste and other recyclable material — with the necessary licences, documentation and regulatory compliances in place.

Where this applies

  • Factories & industrial units
  • Corporate offices & IT parks
  • Warehouses & godowns
  • Project and demolition sites
Enquire about this

Disposal is only straightforward when the paperwork is in order. We take responsibility for the full cycle: assessment of the material lying at your site, agreed rates, segregation, weighment, safe removal and the records that go with it.

The same process applies whether the material is a one-time clearance after a project or a running monthly lift from an operating facility.

Scope of work
  • Ferrous and non-ferrous scrap of all grades
  • Electronic waste collection, handling and channelisation
  • Obsolete plant, machinery and equipment buy-back
  • On-site segregation, weighment and material accounting
  • Licences, documentation and regulatory compliance
  • Safe dismantling, loading and transportation
  • One-time clearance or recurring monthly lifting
All transactions are supported by proper documentation so your disposal record stays audit-ready.
