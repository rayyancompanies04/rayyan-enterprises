02 — Approach

Work that holds up to inspection

Quality, compliance, timely execution, competitive pricing and professional service are the terms we are judged on — so they are the terms we plan around.

More about the firm
01

Site visit and assessment

We inspect the location, confirm scope and access, note the safety constraints and record the material or manpower position before quoting anything.

02

Itemised proposal

A written, line-by-line quotation with the relevant licences and statutory papers attached. What is included and what is not is stated on the document.

03

Planned execution

Teams, supervision and equipment mobilise on an agreed date. Segregation, safety checks and progress reporting run through the job, not at the end of it.

04

Clearance and handover

The site is cleared, material is accounted for, records are handed over, and we stay available for maintenance, repeat lifting or follow-on work.

03 — Why clients stay

Fewer vendors, cleaner paperwork

The reasons clients give us for continuing tend to be practical ones.

Documentation in order

Licences, statutory records and material accounting maintained for every category of work we undertake.
