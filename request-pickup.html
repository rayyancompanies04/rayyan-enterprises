Common questions

What kind of e-waste do you buy?

Do you offer pickup, or do we need to drop off?
We offer doorstep pickup across India for bulk lots. Individuals with small quantities can also arrange a scheduled collection.

Storage media is wiped or physically shredded to NIST 800-88 standards, and every job comes with a signed destruction certificate.
