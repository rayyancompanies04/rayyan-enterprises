Our 4-Step Process

A transparent, certified journey from your loading dock to a fully closed-loop material recovery stream.

No surprises at any stage

From enquiry to payout, mapped out.

Share category, rough quantity and location through our form, WhatsApp or a call. We return a transparent quote, usually within one business day, based on live rates. Pickup slots are scheduled, devices are wiped or shredded, and payout is released with destruction and disposal certificates.

01

Scheduling & Collection

Tailored logistics planning. We dispatch specialized vehicles equipped for the safe handling of sensitive electronics directly to your facility.

02

Secure Transport

GPS-tracked fleet ensures a verifiable chain of custody. Your assets are monitored continuously from origin to our processing center.

03

Data Destruction

NIST 800-88 compliant wiping or physical shredding. We guarantee irrevocable data destruction, backed by an official Certificate of Destruction.

04

Eco-Recycling

Advanced sorting technologies separate raw materials — copper, gold, plastics — for reintroduction into global supply chains, minimizing landfill impact.

Next-Gen Sorting Tech

Our facility employs AI-driven optical sorters and advanced shredding arrays to achieve industry-leading material purity rates. We process complex e-waste streams with surgical precision.
