Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services, processes, and what to expect when working with Rayyan Enterprises.

General

About our services

Questions clients frequently ask before engaging us.

What services do you offer?

We offer seven service verticals: Scrap & E-Waste Management, Demolition Work, Labour Supply, Fencing Work, Interior Work, Glass & Wallpaper Work, and Civil Work. Most clients engage us across multiple verticals for coordinated project execution.

Do you handle residential projects?

Yes, we work with residential clients including apartments, villas, and housing societies for interior work, civil repairs, wallpaper installation, glass work, and restoration projects.

What is your service area?

We operate primarily in [your city/region] and surrounding areas. For larger projects or specific requirements, we can discuss service area extensions on a case-by-case basis.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, we maintain all necessary licences for each service vertical, particularly for scrap and e-waste management where regulatory compliance is critical. We also carry appropriate insurance coverage.

Process

How we work

Questions about our working methodology and project execution.

How do I get a quote?

Contact us via phone, email, or the contact form on our website. We will schedule a site visit to assess the scope, access, and requirements before providing a detailed, itemised quotation.

What is included in your quotation?

Our quotations are itemised line-by-line, clearly stating what is included and what is not. We attach relevant licences and statutory documents where applicable, so you have complete visibility.

How long does a typical project take?

Timeline varies by scope and complexity. We provide realistic schedules based on actual mobilisation capacity and communicate any changes in writing if site conditions require adjustments.

Do you provide documentation?

Yes, particularly for scrap and e-waste management. We provide disposal records, weighment receipts, and all necessary regulatory documentation to keep your records audit-ready.

Pricing & Payment

Commercial terms

Questions about pricing, payment terms, and commercial arrangements.

How do you price your services?

We provide competitive, itemised pricing quoted up front. Rates are held for the agreed quotation period, and we do not introduce additional charges mid-project without written agreement.

What are your payment terms?

Payment terms are typically structured around project milestones. Specific terms are detailed in our quotation and agreement, tailored to the nature and duration of the work.

Do you charge for site visits?

Initial site visits for assessment and quotation are typically complimentary. We assess the scope, access, and requirements to provide an accurate quote without obligation.

Are there hidden charges?

No. Our itemised quotations clearly state what is included. Any changes to scope or additional work are documented and agreed upon in writing before execution.

Still have questions?

We're happy to help clarify anything else.

If you didn't find the answer you were looking for, reach out to us directly and we'll address your specific requirements.
